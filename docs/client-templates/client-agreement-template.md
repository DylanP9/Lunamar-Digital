## 6. Minimum term

- Website services: **12-month minimum term.**
- Social media add-ons (where included): **3-month minimum term.**

## 7. Billing start date

Billing begins **30 days after onboarding / the Start Date**, even if the Client has not yet supplied content or assets.

## 8. Payment terms

- The monthly fee is payable in advance by [Direct Debit / standing order / card] on the [same day each month].
- The first payment is due on [date].

## 9. Late payment

- Invoices/payments are due on the stated date.
- If payment is more than [7] days late, we may pause services until the account is brought up to date.
- Repeated or prolonged non-payment ([30] days+) may be treated as a breach and grounds for suspension or termination, without waiving amounts owed for the remaining minimum term.

## 10. Client responsibilities

- Provide accurate business information and timely approvals.
- Provide content and assets needed for the work (see clause 11).
- Nominate one main point of contact for approvals.

## 11. Asset & content supply

- The Client supplies logos, photos, videos, copy, menus, prices, opening hours and other business content required.
- Social media content is created from Client-supplied photos/videos unless a content visit/shoot is quoted separately.

## 12. Delayed content

- Billing and the minimum term run from the dates above regardless of content delays.
- If required content is outstanding for an extended period, launch and delivery dates move accordingly; fees remain payable.

## 13. Small updates definition

"Small updates" means reasonable content changes such as text edits, image swaps, price changes, opening-hour changes, service updates, link updates and small promotional changes.

## 14. Update limits / fair-use

- Small updates are limited to approximately **2 hours per month** unless the selected package states otherwise.
- Unused time does not roll over. There is **no unlimited updates** offer.

## 15. Revisions

- Reasonable revisions are included during the build at agreed milestones.
- Substantial change requests after sign-off of a milestone may be quoted separately.

## 16. Excluded work (quoted separately)

Major redesigns, new pages, rebrands, booking systems, ordering systems, payment systems, custom integrations, content shoots/photography days, and paid ad management are **not** included in the monthly fee and are scoped and quoted separately.

## 17. Social media boundaries

- Applies to packages that include social media only.
- Content is produced from supplied assets and scheduled to agreed platforms.
- We do not guarantee follower counts, reach or engagement.

## 18. Content approval process

- Social posts are shared for approval in batches.
- The Client has **72 hours** to request edits to scheduled content; if no response is received, posts may be scheduled as prepared.

## 19. Domain ownership

The Client owns their domain name. We can help register and connect it. Where we register on the Client's behalf, it is held for and transferable to the Client.

## 20. Hosting responsibility

Hosting support is included for the duration of an active plan. If the plan ends, hosting arrangements will be discussed and may require transfer to a Client-owned account.

## 21. Website / code ownership

The Client owns their content, logo and photos at all times. **Lunamar owns the website design and code until the 12-month minimum term is completed or a buyout/transfer fee is paid** (see clause 23).

## 22. Early cancellation

If the Client cancels before the end of the minimum term, the remaining contract balance for the minimum term, or an agreed buyout/termination fee, becomes payable.

## 23. Buyout / transfer fee

- A buyout/transfer fee of £[amount] (or the remaining minimum-term balance) may be paid to take ownership of the website design and code before the end of the term.
- On payment, relevant files/access are transferred within [14] days.

## 24. Intellectual property

- Client materials (logo, photos, copy, brand) remain the Client's property.
- Third-party assets (fonts, stock, libraries) remain subject to their own licences.
- Lunamar retains ownership of the site build per clauses 21–23.

## 25. Third-party services

Some features rely on third-party services (e.g. hosting, analytics, payment providers such as Square). Their availability, terms and any fees are outside Lunamar's control.

## 26. Payment / order systems (if applicable)

Ordering, booking and payment functionality (e.g. Square checkout) is scoped and quoted separately. The Client is responsible for their own merchant accounts, fees and compliance.

Each party will keep the other's non-public business information confidential and use it only to deliver the services.
